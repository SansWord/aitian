      en: |
        Before thinking about what AI can help with, I care more about asking: “How do I actually work?”

        Over more than two years of producing Initium Media's Initium Reports Podcast, we broke each episode down into multiple stages, reorganized workflows scattered across different platforms and team members, and gradually made nearly half of those stages fully or semi-automated using Google Apps Script, AI, and other tools. On the editing side, we also developed an automated workflow spanning script formatting, asset tagging, and rough cuts.

        This talk approaches the process from both the producer's and editor's perspectives, sharing how we turned the day-to-day work of a news podcast into a workflow that systems can understand—and how using AI helped us reconsider which tasks are suitable for AI, and which should remain with people because they depend on experience and judgment.
      zh: |
        在思考 AI 能幫上什麼忙之前，我更在意的是「我到底怎麼工作？」

        過去兩年多製作端傳媒 Podcast「端聞」期間，我們把一集節目細拆成多個工作環節，重新整理散落在不同平台與團隊成員之間的工作流，再利用 Google Apps Script、AI 與各種工具，逐步將近一半的環節改為全自動或半自動化。剪輯端也從腳本格式、素材標記到初剪，發展出一套自動化流程。

        這次分享會從製作人與剪輯師兩端出發，分享我們如何把一檔新聞 Podcast 的日常工作整理成可以被系統理解的流程，以及在運用 AI 的過程中，如何重新確認哪些工作環節適合交給 AI，哪些事情即使可以自動化，仍選擇保留給人的經驗與判斷。
