      zh: |
        當 AI 什麼都能翻譯，學習語言更關乎個人表達（expression）：幫學習者找到自己的話。Lingocubes 從這個理念出發，不管是一首歌、Netflix 影集裡聽到的一段對話，還是一張菜單的照片，都能變成有聲方塊，讓你把玩、聆聽、拆解、練習跟著說。我會分享身為一個語言學習狂，本來只是想幫自己做個記憶字卡 app，結果一路做到現在的故事。我的日語說得比台語好，一直讓我很愧疚，於是後來我也做了 TaigiCubes。原來想用 AI 寫台語，不一定需要專門的台語 LLM。
      en: |
        When AI can translate anything, the future of language learning is about expression: helping people find their own words. Lingocubes is our attempt at building that. Whether it's a song, a conversation you heard in a Netflix show, or a photo of a menu, each is turned into audio cubes you can tap, listen to, practice, and shadow. This talk is the story of how a language-learning maniac set out to build a flashcard app for himself and ended up here. I felt guilty that my Taiwanese (Taigi) was worse than the new languages I kept picking up, so I turned to TaigiCubes. What I learned: you don't need an LLM trained on a dedicated Taigi corpus to write Taigi.
