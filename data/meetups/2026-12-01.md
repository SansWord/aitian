      zh: |
        十月，CL 讓 coding agent 把 3D 模型變成樂高組：一棟 4,074 片的建築、一台 348 片的載具（已公開為 [DSS Taipei Current 組裝說明書](https://clkao.github.io/dss-taipei-current/)），以及一個以台北街區為藍本的積木模型 Studcity。幾何結構、說明書和零件清單都由 agent 產出，而真正讓成果變好的，是有人看著渲染圖說「不行」。

        有一次審查放過了一張劍浮在半空中的劍王座，於是流程現在要求證明每個零件都確實接上；有位玩家在已公開的說明書裡發現一個會卡關的步驟，隔天就推出修正版。每一次「退回重做」，都成了下一次建造必須通過的規則。

        現場你會連上 Studcity，挑一棟建築讓你的 agent 去蓋，再親自審查它的成果。這套樂高設計與說明書工作流程是用 [Spacedock](https://spacedock.md/) 打造的。
      en: |
        In October CL had coding agents turn 3D models into Lego sets: a 4,074-piece building, a 348-piece vehicle published as the [DSS Taipei Current instructions](https://clkao.github.io/dss-taipei-current/), and a brick model of a Taipei neighbourhood called Studcity. The agents made the geometry, the manuals and the parts lists. What made the results better was a person looking at a render and saying no.

        A review passed a sword throne whose swords were floating, so the workflow now asks for proof that each part is attached. A builder found a trap in the published manual, and a corrected edition came out the next day. Each "send it back" became a rule that the next build had to meet.

        At the meetup you connect to Studcity, pick a building for your agent to build, and review what it makes. The Lego design and instruction manual workflows are built with [Spacedock](https://spacedock.md/).
