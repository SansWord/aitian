      zh: |
        分享將從參與中央社 MCP 開發的第一線經驗出發，介紹我們如何把中央社累積的新聞資料，轉化成 AI 可以理解與調用的工具。過程將先簡介 MCP 的核心概念、中央社 MCP 的功能與設計思考，以及實作過程中遇到的問題與取捨；也會透過實際案例，展示如何運用 MCP 進行新聞搜尋、資料整理與視覺化。此外也將分享中央社 MCP 的延伸應用，包含翻譯與短影音製作。
      en: |
        Drawing on first-hand experience developing CNA's MCP, this talk will explore how we transformed CNA's accumulated news data into tools that AI can understand and call. We will introduce the core concepts of MCP, the features and design thinking behind CNA's MCP, and the problems and trade-offs encountered during implementation. Through real-world examples, we will also demonstrate how MCP can be used for news search, data organization, and visualization, before sharing extensions such as translation and short-form video production.
